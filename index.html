Money Factor Converter


When leasing a car, your monthly payment is influenced by several key factors:

  • Capitalized Cost (Cap Cost): The agreed-upon price of the car at the lease's start.
  • Residual Value: The estimated value of the car at the end of the lease term, set by the leasing company.
  • Lease Term: The duration of the lease, typically ranging from 24 to 48 months.
  • Money Factor: The interest rate on the lease, expressed as a small decimal (e.g., 0.0025 instead of 6% APR).


The Money Factor is a crucial component of a car lease, representing the interest rate. It's typically expressed as a small decimal (e.g., 0.00250). This tool helps you easily convert between the Money Factor and its equivalent Annual Percentage Rate (APR), making lease calculations clearer and more transparent dynamically.
